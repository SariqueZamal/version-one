About Elmdon Roofing Services

15+ years of verified roofing trade experience, operating with pride from our local base in Sheldon / Elmdon, Birmingham.

Our Story & Heritage

Local Trade Excellence Across Birmingham & Solihull

Based at 142 Manor House Lane, Birmingham (B26 1PS), Elmdon Roofing Services has built its reputation on straightforward, honest workmanship. With over 15 years in the roofing trade, our team has repaired and replaced roofs on every kind of Midlands property — from 1930s suburban semi-detached homes to Victorian slate terraces and modern commercial flat roofs.

We understand that hiring a roofing contractor can feel daunting for homeowners worried about rogue traders and inflated estimates. That is why we operate with total transparency: 100% free inspections, written fixed quotes, and comprehensive 10 to 20-year guarantees on all major work.

Our Standards

The Principles We Stand By

Every job, whether a single slipped tile or a complete multi-storey roof replacement, is carried out to strict British construction standards.

No High-Pressure Sales

We never pressurise you into unneeded work. If a straightforward £200 tile repair resolves your leak, that is precisely what we will recommend.

Clean & Respectful Sites

We treat your home as if it were our own. Protective sheeting is laid, debris is safely disposed of, and driveways are magnetically swept for stray nails.

Written Workmanship Guarantees

On project completion, you receive signed written documentation confirming your warranty terms (10 to 20 years for re-roofs and EPDM systems).
