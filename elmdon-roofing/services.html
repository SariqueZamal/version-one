Materials Comparison

Choosing The Right Roofing Material

We advise homeowners objectively on the longevity, cost, and structural requirements of each roofing system.

Concrete Interlocking Tiles

Lifespan: 40–50+ Years
Extremely cost-effective, heavy-duty, and weather-resilient. Ideal for modern Birmingham semi-detached and terraced homes. Available in red, brown, and anthracite grey.

Natural Slate Roofing

Lifespan: 75–100+ Years
The pinnacle of British architectural beauty. Fireproof, waterproof, and impervious to frost. Perfect for period properties in Moseley, Edgbaston, and Sutton Coldfield.

Single-Ply EPDM Rubber

Lifespan: 30–50 Years
The gold standard for flat roofs. Does not split, crack, or degrade under direct sunlight. Cold applied with no open flames, making it exceptionally safe for domestic installations.
