Facing a Roofing Issue on Your Property?

Unaddressed roof faults quickly lead to ruined ceilings, decayed rafters, and costly internal damp. Here is how our Birmingham team resolves common roofing headaches.

Water Stains & Active Leaks?

Notice brown water stains on upstairs ceilings or drips during heavy rainfall? Water may be entering from cracked cement valleys or slipped tiles.

Elmdon Solution
Same-day pinpoint leak detection, tile resealing, and waterproof lead renewal.

Missing or Slipped Tiles?

Broken tiles in the garden or dislodged slates after strong winds? Exposed timber underlay rots rapidly if left open to British weather.

Elmdon Solution
Direct matching tile replacements securely fastened with modern dry fix clamps.

Ponding on Flat Roofs?

Traditional mineral felt on garage or extension roofs cracks, blisters, and pools water after 10–12 years, leading to sudden water ingress.

Elmdon Solution
Single-ply seamless EPDM rubber roofing systems with zero joints and 20-year durability.

Chimney Stack Decay?

Cracking mortar on chimney brickwork and degraded lead flashings can collapse or cause continuous moisture to run down internal chimney breasts.

Elmdon Solution
Full raking out and repointing, new Code 4 stepped lead flashing, and secure pot flaunching.
