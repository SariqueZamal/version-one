Measurable Environmental Science

WHY GREEN ROOFS?
THE FACTS SPEAK.

Rigorous environmental benefits backed by genuine scientific studies, building physics, and UK urban planning requirements.

80%

Stormwater Management

Engineered substrate and acicular drainage retain up to 80% of annual stormwater runoff, attenuating peak sewer discharge and preventing flash flooding in urban areas.

3x

Extended Roof Lifespan

Shields the waterproof membrane from damaging UV radiation, hail, and extreme diurnal temperature swings, extending expected rooftop life from 15 to 40+ years.

-10%

Energy Consumption

Studies prove winter heating energy can be reduced by over 10%, while summer evapotranspiration delivers natural cooling, drastically curbing AC demand.

50 dB

Acoustic Sound Insulation

The porous substrate, living plant matter, and trapped air cavities act as high-performance sound dampening, reducing external aircraft and traffic noise by up to 50 dB.

25 °C

Enhanced Solar PV Output

Solar panels lose efficiency when surface temperatures exceed 25°C. Plant transpiration keeps the rooftop ambient temperature optimal, boosting solar power generation.

172+

Verified Urban Species

Scientific rooftop surveys found over 172 separate insect and plant species flourishing on urban green roofs, re-establishing critical corridors for endangered solitary bees.
