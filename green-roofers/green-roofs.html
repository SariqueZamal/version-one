Technical Classification & Build-Ups

TYPES OF GREEN ROOFS

Green roofs come in diverse architectural forms, individually specified to meet structural weight constraints, ecological planning policies (UGF & BNG), and aesthetic goals.

Most Popular Specification

01 — Extensive Green Roofs

Extensive green roofs provide a lightweight, low-maintenance, instant "green effect" solution, and represent our most common commercial and residential installation.

Extensive roofs are not usually intended for public pedestrian traffic and are chosen primarily for ecological, thermal, and rainwater mitigation benefits. Typically utilising sedum or wildflower vegetation due to their resilience in harsh rooftop climates and lightweight structural requirements (70 – 110 kg/m² saturated).

Planting Variants: Pre-cultivated Sedum Blankets, Hand-Planted Plugs, or Cost-Effective Hydrosprayed Seed Mixes.

Rich Biodiversity & Aesthetics

02 — Semi-Intensive Green Roofs

Semi-intensive systems incorporate a deeper, richer engineered substrate (120 – 200 mm) enabling the cultivation of a wider, more complex range of plant mixtures.

Commonly specified in highly visible areas such as podium decks and overlooked residential roofs. Incorporates small flowering shrubs, ornamental forbs, and drought-tolerant grasses, creating a rich meadow aesthetic with moderate seasonal maintenance.

Load Capacity: Typically 130 – 220 kg/m² saturated. Exceptional rainwater attenuation and floral diversity.

Targeted BNG & Planning Compliance

03 — Biodiverse & Brown Roofs

Biodiverse roofs are engineered primarily for specific ecological benefits, designed to recreate habitats in urban settings that would otherwise be lost to ground-level construction.

Often referred to as brown roofs, these systems feature varied substrate topographies, crushed recycled brick, gravel, sand pockets, and deadwood logs to encourage colonisation by solitary bees, rare spiders, and local bird species such as the Black Redstart. Essential for accelerating UK planning approvals and satisfying local Biodiversity Net Gain requirements.

Amenity & Rooftop Parks

04 — Intensive Green Roof Gardens

Intensive green roofs replicate what is typically found at ground level in natural parks and luxury cultivated gardens.

Involves deep landscaping (200 – 500+ mm substrate) designed for regular human access, outdoor dining, and communal wellness. Accommodates lawns, heavy shrubs, perennials, and small trees, complete with integrated pathways, lighting, and automated irrigation.

Renewable Energy Synergy

05 — Biosolar Green Roofs

The ultimate commercial dual-benefit rooftop: combining solar photovoltaic power generation with extensive living sedum blankets.

Solar panels operate at peak electrical conversion efficiency at approximately 25°C. Conventional dark roofs reach 70°C in summer, significantly reducing PV output. The natural cooling evapotranspiration of our sedum keeps panels within optimal operating temperature, boosting solar energy generation by up to 12%.
