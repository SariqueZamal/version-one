01 / SPECIFICATION & CONSULTATION

Ecologically-Led Design & Consultation

Our process starts by listening to our clients — understanding their needs and aspirations, then producing scientific, ecologically-led solutions uniquely tailored to their context, building geometry, and the needs of the end user.

We provide architects and developers with precise structural dead/saturated load calculations, plant species palettes suited for local solar exposure, and detailed technical CAD details for flashing, drainage outlets, and parapet interfaces.

  • Urban Greening Factor (UGF) & BREEAM Credit Optimisation
  • Structural Weight Capacity & Retention Sizing
  • Biodiversity Net Gain (BNG) Native Planting Palettes
  • CAD & BIM Technical Construction Details

03 / PRECISION INSTALLATION

Certified Installation & Site Safety

Our installation teams are trained and experienced in delivering complex green roof systems across challenging architectural sites. We understand construction logistics and follow strict guidelines to ensure lasting sustainability.

Working at height demands uncompromising standards. We coordinate directly with Health & Safety Executives (HSE), principal building contractors, and structural engineers, working strictly within UK Construction (Design and Management) CDM regulations.

  • Full CDM Compliance & Safe Working at Height Training
  • Pre-Installation Electronic Vector Leak Testing
  • Crane Logistics & Controlled Material Distribution
  • Pitched Slope Stabilisation & Geocellular Grids
