ABOUT GREEN ROOFERS

Designers, suppliers and certified installers of living roof systems. Striving for healthier, more liveable, resilient British cities.

Our Philosophy

Bringing Nature Back Into the Built Environment

At Green Roofers Ltd, we specialise in providing sustainable green roof solutions. We design, supply, and install a range of green roof systems that are individually crafted to suit every project's unique needs.

We have a passionate team of designers, planners, horticulturalists, and installers dedicated to ensuring that every green roof project is delivered on time, within budget, and with a service that exceeds the highest expectations.

We understand the growing need for green space within our rapidly expanding urban environment, and provide green roof solutions to meet any challenge. We believe that green solutions provide a vital role in building an environmentally friendly culture, enhancing the modern landscape in a positive, clean, and visually inspiring way.

"We are very proud to be part of a movement that is striving for more liveable, healthy cities."
Continuous Evolution

Our Ethos: Research & Development

At Green Roofers our first thought is how to ensure the sustainability of our projects. That is why we continue to research and evolve our process, and the products we use.

Our research and development process has evolved gradually, learning from each design to meet the demands of each project and clients' specifications. Through careful evaluation of each site, we aim to drive a good design through to completion into something that is extraordinary, sustainable, and completely unique.

Uncompromising Standards

On-Site Safety & CDM Compliance

When working on a project, our first priority is always safety. It is especially evident when working at height. We work with the Health and Safety Executive (HSE), architects, building contractors, and property owners to ensure all safety standards are met. All of our team members are trained to the latest safety standards, certified in safety equipment, and work strictly within UK CDM (Construction Design and Management) regulations.

Working at Height Certified fall-arrest, edge protection, and harness rigging.
CDM Regulations Full health & safety documentation, risk assessments and method statements (RAMS).
Pre-Install Leak Testing Electronic vector leak testing guarantees membrane integrity.
