Material Science & Structural Craft

A roof envelope is only as resilient as its weakest component. We strictly specify certified Tier-1 materials engineered to outlast extreme UK climate cycles.

ENVELOPE ANATOMY

The 6-Layer Architectural Build-Up

A high-performance roof is an engineered system of thermal barriers, air voids, and moisture management layers working in unison.

LAYER 06 · EXTERNAL WEATHER COVERING

Welsh Penrhyn Slate or Architectural Clay Tiles

Impermeable metamorphic stone or high-fired ceramic tiles mechanically fixed with copper ring-shank nails or stainless steel clips to withstand BS 6399 wind uplift forces.

100+ Year Lifespan
LAYER 05 · STRUCTURAL FIXING BATTENS

BS 5534 Graded Timber Battens (25x50mm)

Independently factory-graded softwood stamped with BS 5534 certification, treated with preservative salts, providing the exact gauge and structural anchor points.

Independently Graded
LAYER 04 · COUNTER-BATTEN VENTILATION VOID

Continuous Air Drainage Cavity (25mm)

Permits an uninterrupted cross-flow of air beneath the slate covering, discharging interstitial moisture and preventing heat buildup during high summer ambient temperatures.

BS 5250 Ventilation
LAYER 03 · SECONDARY WATERPROOF DEFENSE

High-Tensile Vapour-Permeable Membrane

Micro-porous spunbond composite underlay allowing attic water vapour to diffuse outward while completely preventing wind-driven snow and driving rain from penetrating the rafters.

Zero Wind Uplift Flap
LAYER 02 · THERMAL CORE & VAPOUR CONTROL

Rigid PIR Insulation & Foil VCL

High-density polyisocyanurate insulation achieving exceptional thermal performance (0.16 W/m²K U-value) coupled with an internal continuous vapour control layer preventing interstitial condensation.

Part L Compliant
LAYER 01 · STRUCTURAL SKELETON

C24 Pressure-Impregnated Softwood Rafters

Kiln-dried timber engineered for high load-bearing capacity, treated with anti-fungal preservatives and secured with structural steel truss ties and wall plate straps.

C24 Strength Class
COMPONENT PROVENANCE

The Master Materials

Welsh Slate (Penrhyn Quarry)

North Wales · Metamorphic S1/T1 Grade

Widely recognized as the finest roofing slate on earth. Formed 500 million years ago, Welsh slate has zero chemical porosity and does not delaminate, fade, or soften over centuries of precipitation.

• Water Absorption: 0.12% (Near Zero) • Bending Strength: Exceptional (T1 Grade) • Colour Stability: Permanent Heather Purple / Dark Blue • Warranty Profile: 100+ Year Historic Record

Milled Lead (Code 4, 5, 6)

BS EN 12588 Certified Milled Sheet

Machine-rolled to consistent grain structure and thickness, preventing pinholes and cracking under thermal cycling. Extensively dressed and welded to form impermeable flashings and gutters.

• Code 4 (1.80mm): Soakers, Flashings, Upstands • Code 5 (2.24mm): Valleys, Dormers, Parapets • Code 6 (2.65mm): Commercial Box Gutters • Treatment: Patination Oil to Prevent Carbonation

Carlisle 1.52mm EPDM Rubber

BBA Agrément Certificate 92/2791

High-performance synthetic elastomeric membrane engineered for extreme durability. Remains fully flexible at -45°C and withstands ponding water, standing snow, and intense solar radiation without degradation.

• Sheet Width: Up to 15.25m Monolithic • Tensile Elongation: > 300% • Fire Classification: Broof(t4) Certified • Field Life Expectancy: 50+ Years
