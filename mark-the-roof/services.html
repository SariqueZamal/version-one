Chimney Services

Chimney Repairs & Pots Replaced

Exposed chimney stacks suffer extreme freeze-thaw weathering. Mark specialises in deep structural mortar repointing, rebuilding decayed brick courses, and replacing loosened terracotta pots with proper flaunching.

  • ✓ Terracotta Chimney Pots: Securely bedded in high-adhesion cement flaunching.
  • ✓ Chimney Repointing: Defective mortar raked out cleanly and repointed flush.
  • ✓ Flue Capping: Cowls and rain guards installed on disused flues.

Lead Flashings, Lead Valleys & Guttering
